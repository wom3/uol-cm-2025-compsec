# Career development plan

I am currently a career starter in cybersecurity. I am still building my technical foundation, but I already have useful transferable skills from study and teamwork, including problem-solving, communication, critical thinking and organisation. My strengths include learning quickly, analysing information and working with others to solve problems. To move into cybersecurity, I need to strengthen my understanding of networking, encryption, secure coding and ethical decision-making, while also improving my ability to communicate technical issues clearly to non-technical audiences.

## Short-term goals (1–2 years)

My immediate aim is to build a solid base in core cyber concepts and secure entry-level opportunities. I plan to complete an introductory certification such as CompTIA Security+ or a cloud fundamentals qualification, because this will improve my employability and give me recognised evidence of my knowledge. I also want to improve my practical skills through labs, online challenges and guided projects, focusing on networking, operating systems, malware awareness and secure coding practices. In addition, I will strengthen my global employability skills by presenting technical findings clearly, working in teams and responding ethically to real-world scenarios. My short-term target is to gain an internship, work placement or trainee role in IT security, data protection or digital operations.

## Medium-term goals (3–5 years)

After gaining experience, I would like to work in a role such as SOC analyst, junior security engineer or cloud security specialist. To support this, I will pursue relevant professional development such as CySA+, Azure or AWS security certifications, or a cyber-focused postgraduate module if needed. I also plan to join a professional body such as BCS or (ISC)² and participate in relevant communities, events and networking opportunities. These steps will help me develop specialist knowledge, build confidence and move from generalist cyber awareness into a skilled practitioner role.

## Long-term goals (5+ years)

In the longer term, I hope to progress into a senior role such as security architect, team lead or policy advisor. I want to maintain sustainability and employability by continuing to learn, staying current with emerging threats and embracing inclusive leadership. This means working ethically, helping to build diverse teams and using clear communication to promote good practice across organisations. I also want to mentor others and contribute to a strong security culture in my future workplace.

## Milestones and resources

Over the next few years, I will work towards the following milestones:

1. Complete a foundational certification such as Security+ or cloud fundamentals.
2. Build a portfolio on GitHub with a secure application, network lab or CTF-style project.
3. Attend a University of London Careers Service panel or a local cybersecurity event.
4. Join a professional network or industry body and seek mentor support.
5. Present a technical project to a non-specialist audience to strengthen communication.

Resources I will use include the University of London Careers Service, online platforms such as TryHackMe, GitHub and Microsoft Learn, virtual internships, Forage experiences, cybersecurity communities and Capture the Flag competitions. These activities will support my technical growth, professional confidence and readiness for the cybersecurity profession.
